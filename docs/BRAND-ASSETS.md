# BRAND-ASSETS.md — the icon, and everywhere the site shows up outside a tab

Nobody needs to read this to work on the site. Read it when the mark changes,
or when something the site sends elsewhere — a shared link, a saved home-screen
icon — looks wrong.

## The one source

Everything here comes from **`app/icon.svg`**, except the link-preview card,
which comes from **`public/brand/horizon-wordmark.svg`**. Both are the team's
own artwork. Nothing is redrawn.

`app/icon.svg` is **the team logo**: `public/brand/horizon-mark-gradient.svg`,
the logo on the sponsorship package cover. Measured against the cover, the
horizon is 2.5 times the ring's width, the ring's stroke is 8% of it and the
horizon's thickness 11% — identical in both. It differs from that file in
exactly one way, the viewBox. The supplied artboard is 1200 × 1200 with a lot of
empty space around the logo; the icon crops to the logo's own bounds, with a
small margin past the horizon's tips, and squares the box because a favicon
slot is square. Every path, gradient and stop is byte-for-byte the supplied
file. It ships transparent.

**The whole logo stays in frame, including the full horizon.** An earlier
version cropped to the sun and cut the horizon to a stub just wider than the
ring. That made it bolder at 16px and also made it stop looking like the logo.
A long, shallow horizon under the sun is what the logo is.

**Not** `public/brand/horizon-logo-gradient.svg`. Despite the name, that is a
different drawing: a thinner ring on a horizon more than four times its width.
It does not match the team's own material.

The logo is a little over twice as wide as it is tall, so at a 16px tab it is
small. That is the cost of using it unaltered.

## What gets generated, and what reads it

| File                                 | Size      | Read by                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | --------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `app/favicon.ico`                    | 16–48     | Safari, which ignores SVG icons entirely     |
| `app/apple-icon.png`                 | 180 × 180 | iOS home screen, Safari bookmarks            |
| `public/icons/icon-192.png`          | 192 × 192 | Android install prompt                       |
| `public/icons/icon-512.png`          | 512 × 512 | Android splash screen                        |
| `public/icons/icon-maskable-512.png` | 512 × 512 | Android, cropped to the launcher's own shape |
| `app/opengraph-image.jpg`            | 1200× 630 | Slack, Discord, iMessage, X, Facebook        |

`app/icon.svg` itself is what Chrome, Firefox and Edge use. The `.ico` is only
a fallback; browsers pick whichever they understand, so adding it took nothing
away from the ones already working.

The three files under `app/` are picked up by Next by filename, and it writes
the `<link>` and `<meta>` tags itself. The manifest is generated by
`app/manifest.ts` and served at `/manifest.webmanifest`.

Two of the sizes are not the logo at full bleed:

- The **home-screen and install icons** sit on the brand ground at 88% of the
  tile. The logo's ends are at mid-height, far from the corners iOS and
  Android round off, so it can run close to the edges.
- The **maskable icon** is at 74%, because Android may crop it to a circle, a
  squircle or a teardrop, and only the middle 80% is guaranteed to survive.
  The horizon's two tips are the points farthest from the centre, and at 74%
  they land just inside that circle.

## Regenerating

After editing `app/icon.svg` or the wordmark:

```powershell
pnpm add -D puppeteer-core; node scripts/render-brand-assets.mjs; pnpm remove puppeteer-core
```

The renderer is installed for the one run and taken straight back out, so
`package.json` and the lockfile end up exactly as they started. It needs Chrome
on the machine; set `CHROME` to the binary if it is not in the usual place.

Then look at what changed and commit the images.

Every size is rendered natively at that size rather than shrunk down from one
big image, which is the whole point at 16 pixels: the small ones get the
renderer's own hinting instead of resampling mush.

## The part that fails silently

Link previews need absolute URLs, so `metadataBase` in `app/layout.tsx` has to
be set, and it reads the domain from `site.url` in `lib/site.ts`. If the domain
in `public/CNAME` ever changes, change `site.url` with it. Get this wrong and
nothing breaks visibly — the build carries on and every shared link just shows
a card with no picture.

## Checking it by hand

- **Tab icon** — open the site and look at the tab. Also check a light tab
  strip, which is what most people have.
- **Link preview** — paste the address into a Discord or Slack message box and
  wait for the card to appear. Both re-fetch on their own schedule, so an old
  card can hang around for a day after a change.
- **iPhone** — Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android** — Chrome's menu offers Install app once the manifest is valid.
